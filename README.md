# Tape deck

An ad-free cassette-style music player for your own music files. It runs in the browser and installs to your phone's home screen.

- Tap a reel to play or pause, and drag a reel round to wind the tape.
- Press **load** to add songs from your phone. They're saved on that device and never uploaded anywhere.
- Rename the playlist from the list.

Live at: https://chazamon.github.io/Tape-Dexk/
